# 🎨 Tracing Worksheet Kids

Interactive and modern tracing worksheets designed for children aged **2-5 years**. Print-ready PDFs and digital worksheets to help develop fine motor skills and letter/shape recognition.

## 📋 Contents

### Tracing Activities
- **Letters (A-Z)** - Letter recognition and tracing
- **Numbers (0-10)** - Number tracing and counting
- **Shapes** - Circle, Square, Triangle, Rectangle tracing
- **Lines** - Straight, wavy, and curved line tracing
- **Objects** - Common objects like apple, house, sun

## 🎯 Target Age Group
- **2-3 years**: Basic shapes and lines
- **3-4 years**: Letters and simple objects
- **4-5 years**: Complex patterns and words

1. Open `index.html` in your browser

### Printing
1. Go to the "Printable" section
2. Download PDF files
3. Print on A4/Letter size paper
